Privacy Policy

AI Celebrity keeps your chats on your iPhone

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version: AI Celebrity doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your characters and chats stay on your iPhone.

Who we are

AI Celebrity is an app made by SemantiNote. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean SemantiNote. This policy applies to the AI Celebrity iPhone app.

Your characters and chats

The characters you create, their personality prompts, and everything you chat about (including group chats) are stored only on your iPhone (and in your own iCloud or computer backups, if you use them). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing a chat, deleting a character or deleting the app removes that data.

How replies are written

Replies are written by Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone. Your messages and the character's personality are given to Apple's on-device model only to write the reply. AI Celebrity doesn't send them anywhere else.

Voice mode

Voice mode uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into text, and only while voice mode is open. Whenever your iPhone supports it, this happens entirely on the device; if it doesn't, Apple processes the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your voice or transcripts, and nothing is recorded or saved as audio. Replies are read aloud with voices built into iOS; the app doesn't clone or imitate anyone's real voice.

Reporting a reply

If you tap Report Reply, your Mail app opens with that reply filled in. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email, and we use it only to look into the report.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether you've started the trial or bought the unlock. The count of your first 40 free messages is kept on your iPhone.

Analytics and tracking

The AI Celebrity app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple, which provides the App Store, payments, Apple Intelligence, speech recognition and the built-in voices under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Data retention and deletion

We don't receive or keep any of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Your data stays on your iPhone until you clear a chat (⋯ › Clear Chat), delete a character (long-press it › Delete) or delete the app. If you have any questions about your data, email us and we'll help.

Your choices

You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in your iPhone's Settings › AI Celebrity. The app keeps working without them; only voice mode is unavailable.

AI characters

Characters in AI Celebrity are fictional, AI-generated personas created by users for entertainment. They are not the real people they may be inspired by, aren't affiliated with or endorsed by them, and their replies may be inaccurate.

Children

AI Celebrity isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: semantinotesupport@gmail.com.
