AI Celebrity help

Create any celebrity character, chat like you're texting them, start group chats and talk out loud, all privately on your iPhone. Made by SemantiNote.

Need help? Email semantinotesupport@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Which iPhones can use AI Celebrity?

AI Celebrity runs on Apple Intelligence, on your iPhone. It needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer (including every iPhone 16 and 17 model and iPhone Air) with Apple Intelligence turned on. The App Store only offers the app to iPhones that support it.

It says "Turn on Apple Intelligence" or that it's still downloading

Open Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri and turn on Apple Intelligence. The first time, your iPhone downloads Apple's model, which can take a while on Wi-Fi with the phone charging. AI Celebrity continues by itself once it's ready.

How much does AI Celebrity cost?

The app is free to download and your first 40 messages are free. After that you can start a free 30-day trial of unlimited chats, group chats and voice mode, then keep them forever with a one-time $3.99 purchase (or the equivalent in your country). There's no subscription and nothing renews.

I got a new iPhone or reinstalled the app

Open Settings in AI Celebrity and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Your trial and one-time unlock belong to your Apple Account, so they come back. Your characters and chats are stored only on the iPhone, so they move to a new iPhone only with an iCloud or computer backup.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the AI Celebrity purchase.

Voice mode isn't hearing me or I can't hear the reply

Allow the microphone and speech recognition in Settings › AI Celebrity. Make sure your ringer volume is up and the iPhone isn't connected to a Bluetooth speaker you didn't mean to use. Replies are read aloud with the voices built into iOS; you can pick a different voice for each character.

A reply was inappropriate

Long-press the message and tap Report Reply to email it to us, or Delete to remove it. Replies are written by Apple Intelligence with Apple's safety guardrails, but AI can still make mistakes.

How do I delete my chats and characters?

In a chat, tap ⋯ › Clear Chat. To remove a character, long-press it on the Celebrities screen and tap Delete. Deleting the app removes everything. AI Celebrity has no servers, so nothing is kept anywhere else. See the privacy policy.

Are these the real celebrities?

No. Characters in AI Celebrity are fictional, AI-generated personas that you create for entertainment. Every chat is labeled "AI parody, not the real person". They aren't affiliated with or endorsed by the people they may be inspired by, and their replies may be inaccurate or made up.
