Privacy Policy

FitPulse keeps your health data on your iPhone

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: FitPulse doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

Data you enter

Your profile (name, age, sex, height, weight and goals), workouts, gym days, cardio, food, weight and sleep logs are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Coach › Settings › Erase all data, removes them.

Meal photos

When you scan a meal, the photo is analyzed by Apple's on-device image recognition or by Apple Intelligence (on your device, or Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which doesn't keep your data). FitPulse doesn't save or upload the photo. Camera and photo access are only used when you start a scan.

AI Coach

The AI Coach uses Apple Intelligence. To personalize its answers, a text summary of your logs is given to Apple's model on your device (or Private Cloud Compute where Apple uses it). You can see exactly what is shared in AI Coach › ⋯ › What your coach knows. FitPulse doesn't send it anywhere else.

Notifications

Gym and bedtime reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, and only if you turn them on.

Purchases

The FitPulse Pro subscription and its free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Analytics and tracking

The FitPulse app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.

Children

FitPulse isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health disclaimer

FitPulse provides general fitness information and estimates. It isn't a medical device and isn't medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program.

Changes and contact
