Privacy Policy

QuorAI keeps your research on your computer

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version: QuorAI has no accounts and no tracking. The AI runs on your own computer, and your chats, watchlist and Second Brain memories stay on your device. The app only goes online to fetch market data, check for updates, and — if you choose to add them — use your own Ollama or Alpaca keys.

Who we are

QuorAI is an app made by SemantiNote. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean SemantiNote. This policy applies to the QuorAI desktop app for Mac and Windows. This website is covered by the SemantiNote Privacy Policy.

No account, no tracking

You don't create an account to use QuorAI, and we don't ask for your name, email or payment details. The QuorAI app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.

AI processing on your device

By default, QuorAI runs AI models on your own computer with its built-in AI engine, or with your existing Ollama if you have it. Your questions, the analysts' debate and the verdicts are processed locally and are never sent to us. The AI model you pick in the setup wizard is downloaded to your computer on first run.

Data stored on your device

Your chats, watchlist, price alerts, settings and — if you turn it on — Second Brain memories (the trades and results you mention in chat) are stored only on your computer. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes them.

Market data

To show prices, charts, fundamentals, news and the market pulse, QuorAI fetches market data from Yahoo Finance. These requests include the ticker symbols being looked up, plus the standard information any internet request carries (such as your IP address). They don't include your chats, memories or keys. Yahoo's handling of these requests is covered by its own privacy policy.

Optional Ollama API key

If you add an Ollama API key to use Ollama cloud models or better web search, the key is stored encrypted on your device and is sent only to Ollama. When you use an Ollama cloud model, the questions and context for that request are processed by Ollama under Ollama's privacy policy. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent to Ollama's cloud.

Web search

When an analyst searches the web for news, the search words are sent to Ollama's web search if you've added an Ollama API key, or otherwise to DuckDuckGo. Web pages the analysts read are fetched directly from those websites. You can turn web search off in the chat at any time.

Optional Alpaca key

If you add your own Alpaca key for real-time day-trading data, the key is stored encrypted on your device and is sent only to Alpaca, to request market data. Alpaca's handling of these requests is covered by its own privacy policy.

Automatic updates

QuorAI checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from there. These checks share only the standard information any internet request carries (such as your IP address and the app version), and are handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy.

Third parties

The only outside services QuorAI talks to are Yahoo Finance (market data), GitHub (updates), DuckDuckGo and the websites an analyst reads (web search, if on), and — only if you add your own keys — Ollama and Alpaca. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we don't receive it in the first place.

Data retention and deletion

We don't receive or keep any of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. You can remove your API keys in the app's settings at any time, and uninstalling QuorAI and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

Children

QuorAI isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not financial advice

QuorAI is for research and education only and is not financial advice. See the Terms of Use.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: semantinotesupport@gmail.com.
