FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is QuorAI free?

Yes — QuorAI is free for now. There’s no account to create and no card to enter. Just download it and start.

Is my data private?

Yes. The AI runs on your own computer, and your chats and Second Brain memories stay on your device. QuorAI connects to the internet to fetch market data, check for updates, and — only if you add them — use your own Ollama or Alpaca keys. See the privacy policy for the details.

Do I need Ollama?

No. QuorAI has a built-in AI engine, so there’s nothing else to install or set up. If you already use Ollama, QuorAI can use your existing Ollama instead.

Which AI model does it use?

You choose. The first time you open QuorAI, the setup wizard helps you pick an AI model and downloads it for you (3–6 GB). You can also use models from your own Ollama, or — with an Ollama API key — Ollama cloud models.

Is this financial advice?

No. QuorAI is for research and education only. Its verdicts are AI-generated, can be wrong, and are no guarantee of future results. You are responsible for your own investment decisions — consider talking to a licensed financial professional before you invest.

Do I need an Alpaca account?

No — it’s optional. Add your own free Alpaca key only if you want real-time day-trading data in the analysis.
