Opening it the first time — this is normal & safe ✅

Is this safe? Yes. SemantiNote is a brand-new indie app, so it isn't “code-signed” by Apple/Microsoft yet (that’s a costly yearly fee we’ll add as we grow). Your computer shows a one-time “unrecognized developer” warning for any new app — it is not a virus scan result. Your notes and AI never leave your device, and every purchase is 100% refundable. Here’s how to open it — you only do this once, then it launches normally forever.

Windows — if you see “Windows protected your PC”

1
Click the small More info link (circled below).
2
Then click Run anyway.

That’s it — SemantiNote opens and never asks again.

macOS — if you see “Apple could not verify…”

1
A box pops up saying it can’t be opened. Just click Done (don’t move to Trash).
2
Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
3
Confirm with your password / Touch ID → SemantiNote opens. It never asks again.
