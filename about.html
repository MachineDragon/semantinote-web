About SemantiNote

Notes that know what you mean.

SemantiNote is a notebook with an AI that understands the meaning of what you write — and it does all of it on your own computer, where your notes stay yours.

Where the name comes from

Semanti + Note.

The first half comes from Ancient Greek. Sēmantikós means “having meaning” or “significant.” It grew out of sêma, “a sign,” the idea that marks and words point to something beyond themselves. It’s the same root that gives English the word semantic: to do with meaning.

σημαντικός
sēmantikós
“having meaning”
σῆμα sêma · a sign→ σημαίνω sēmaínō · to signify→ σημαντικός sēmantikós · having meaning→ semantic · about meaning
Semantic+Note=SemantiNote

Put simply, a SemantiNote is a meaning-based note: a note your AI understands by what it’s about, not just by the letters in it.

What that means in practice

Search for an idea, not the exact words.

Most apps search by matching letters. If you don’t remember the exact words you used, you don’t find the note. SemantiNote looks at the underlying concept. Each note is turned into a kind of fingerprint of its meaning, so notes about the same idea sit close together even when they share no words at all.

Keyword search matches letters

how much do I pay to insure my car
No results. Your note never used the words “insure” or “pay.”

SemantiNote matches meaning

how much do I pay to insure my car
Finds “Geico renewal: $642 every 6 months”, because it’s about the same thing.

Because your notes carry meaning the AI can understand, you can also ask them questions (“what did we decide about the launch date?”), get summaries of long meetings and PDFs, and turn rough notes into clean ones.

Why we built it

Powerful AI, without giving up your privacy.

Notes are some of the most personal things people write: journals, health notes, client meetings, ideas, passwords. Most AI tools work by uploading that writing to a company’s servers, where it can be stored, read and used to train the next model.

We wanted the power of modern AI without that trade. So SemantiNote runs open-source large language models, including Qwen for chat and Nomic Embed for search by meaning, fully on your own computer. The AI comes to your notes. Your notes never go to the AI company.

Runs on your computer

The AI runs locally on your Mac or PC. Your notes aren’t sent to us or to anyone else.

Never used for training

No big company trains its models on your writing, because your writing never reaches one.

Works offline

After a one-time model download during setup, the AI works without the internet.

Your files, not ours

No account and no cloud lock-in. Notes are ordinary files on your drive that you can back up, move or open anywhere.

We believe your thoughts should stay yours. SemantiNote gives you the help of AI while keeping your privacy.
