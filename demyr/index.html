Demyr help

Home workouts, calories, body metrics and sleep, in one iPhone app. Made by SemantiNote.

Need help? Email semantinotesupport@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Demyr Pro › Cancel Subscription, or in Demyr go to Coach › Settings › Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or month ends to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How much does Demyr cost?

Demyr Pro is $3.99 per month (or the equivalent in your country) with a 1-month free trial for new subscribers. It renews automatically until you cancel.

I got a new iPhone or reinstalled the app

Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Coach › Settings, signed in with the same Apple Account. Your logs are stored on the device, so they move to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Demyr purchase.

Why don't I see the AI Coach or food photo scan?

These use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. On other iPhones, every workout, tracker and calculator still works, and food can be added from the built-in food search.

Reminders aren't arriving

Check Settings › Notifications › Demyr and make sure notifications are allowed, then turn the reminder off and on again in Coach › Reminders.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Coach › Settings › Erase all data, or delete the app. Demyr has no servers, so nothing is kept anywhere else. See the privacy policy.

Health disclaimer

Calorie, body fat and other numbers in Demyr are estimates for general fitness, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program.
