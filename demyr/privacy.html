Privacy Policy

Demyr keeps your health data on your iPhone

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: Demyr doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no servers and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Demyr is an app made by SemantiNote. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean SemantiNote. This policy applies to the Demyr iPhone app.

Data you enter

Your profile (name, age, sex, height, weight and goals), workouts, gym days, cardio, food, weight and sleep logs are stored only on your iPhone (and in your own iCloud or computer backups, if you use them). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Coach › Settings › Erase all data, removes them.

Meal photos

When you scan a meal, the photo is analyzed by Apple's on-device image recognition or by Apple Intelligence (on your device, or Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which doesn't keep your data). Demyr doesn't save or upload the photo. Camera and photo access are only used when you start a scan.

AI Coach

The AI Coach uses Apple Intelligence. To personalize its answers, a text summary of your logs is given to Apple's model on your device (or Private Cloud Compute where Apple uses it). You can see exactly what is shared in AI Coach › ⋯ › What your coach knows. Demyr doesn't send it anywhere else.

Notifications

Gym and bedtime reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, and only if you turn them on.

Purchases

The Demyr Pro subscription and its free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Analytics and tracking

The Demyr app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple, which provides the App Store, payments, Apple Intelligence and notifications under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

Data retention and deletion

We don't receive or keep any of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it with Coach › Settings › Erase all data or delete the app. If you have any questions about your data, email us and we'll help.

Your choices

You can turn off camera, photo or notification access at any time in your iPhone's Settings › Demyr. You can also start a new AI Coach chat at any time with AI Coach › ⋯ › New chat. The app keeps working without these permissions; only the features that need them are unavailable.

Children

Demyr isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health disclaimer

Demyr provides general fitness information and estimates. It isn't a medical device and isn't medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: semantinotesupport@gmail.com.
