Encrypted secret safe

A built-in vault for the things you shouldn't paste into a plain note — logins, API keys, license codes. Save a name, username and password together; everything's encrypted on your machine with your operating system's secure keychain, and revealed only when you ask. Free for everyone.

  • Encrypted with the OS keychain (macOS & Windows)
  • Name, username & password — reveal or copy each in one click
  • Optional PIN lock on top of the encryption
  • Kept out of your notes — never indexed or sent to AI

  • Draw your signature, or type it in a script, handwritten or classic style
  • Add text anywhere — pick the size, color or a highlight
  • Fill in a PDF's own form fields, checkboxes and dropdowns

Calendar & reminders

A simple calendar built right in. Add a reminder for an appointment, a bill or a birthday — it pops up with the sound you pick, even when SemantiNote is minimized. Missed one while the app was closed? You'll see it next time you open it.

  • One-time or repeating reminders (every weekday, every Monday…)
  • Snooze for 10 minutes, an hour, or until tomorrow
  • Month, year and "next 7 days" views
  • Choose a sound: chime, bell, alarm, gentle or silent

A real notebook, not a toy

Rich notes & tables

Formatting, highlights, colors, images you can resize, and tables — write real documents, not just plain text.

Any file type

Notes, code, JSON, markdown, plain text — create a file, name it anything, and get the right editor automatically.

Multiple tabs

Open several notes at once in browser-style tabs and flip between them as you work.

Pins & colors

Pin the notes you use most and color-code your folders so your notebook stays organized at a glance.

Password-protect a note

Lock any single note with a password — it's encrypted on disk and only you can open it.

Recent & last edited

Jump straight back to what you just opened or changed — every note shows its date.

Works with Excel

Open and edit CSV spreadsheets in a familiar grid — accents, € signs and Excel's formats are kept exactly, so they open right back in Excel.

Keyboard shortcuts

Browser-style shortcuts on Mac and Windows — switch tabs with Ctrl+Tab, jump with ⌘1–9, reopen a closed tab with ⌘⇧T.

Light on your computer

Nothing runs when SemantiNote is closed, the AI frees its memory when idle, and you pick how much memory it can use.
